ALLSHIELD Social Integration Privacy Notice

Effective August 29, 2026

This notice describes how ALLSHIELD Insurance Group handles information used to connect and manage its official social media accounts through the ALLSHIELD back office.

Information we receive

When an authorized ALLSHIELD administrator connects a social account, the provider may return account identifiers, display names, profile information, authorization scopes, access and refresh tokens, account statistics, and public content or performance data that the administrator has authorized the provider to share.

How we use the information

We use this information to authenticate official ALLSHIELD social accounts, publish or upload authorized content, verify connection health and permissions, measure content performance, and support approved social-media operations. We do not request or store a user's social-media password.

Credential and token security

Developer credentials and provider tokens are handled server-side. Secret values are not returned to ordinary browser clients after storage. Access is limited to authorized ALLSHIELD administrative systems and personnel.

Sharing and sale

ALLSHIELD does not sell social integration data. Information may be transmitted to the applicable social-media provider as necessary to perform the action requested through its API, and may be handled by infrastructure providers used to operate ALLSHIELD systems.

Retention and disconnection

Connection data is retained for as long as needed to operate and audit the authorized integration. Disconnecting a provider from the ALLSHIELD back office removes the active provider connection and associated authorization tokens from the active integration path, subject to legitimate security, audit, and legal retention requirements.

Your choices

Authorized administrators can disconnect a social provider from the ALLSHIELD Social Connection Center. Provider-side permissions can also be revoked from the applicable provider account settings.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be submitted through the contact methods provided on the official ALLSHIELD Insurance Group website.
